4. Relational Database Architecture (11 Core Tables)

PostgreSQL: vidya_examination_db

The schema maintains full relational integrity across all cohorts, academic sessions, longitudinal progressions, backlogs, and audit trails.

1. batches
Academic cohort registries tracking start/end years and 151.0 prescribed credits.
2. students
Permanent demographics, roll numbers, enrollment IDs, and institutional status.
3. courses
INC course catalog specifying credit units, theory/practical, and SGPA inclusion flags.
4. student_semester_registrations
Term examination sittings, SGPA, attempt labels, and delivery states (PROVISIONAL / FINAL).
5. student_course_marks
Internal assessment, Part A/B/C splits, grace marks, letter grades, and exemption flags.
6. cumulative_academic_summary
Longitudinal rollup storing Sem 1-8 credits, SGPAs, percentages, and overall CGPA.
7. student_backlogs
Biannual re-appear tracking engine with partial unique index for active backlogs.
8. app_users
RBAC accounts with bcrypt hashing, brute-force lockout, and token_version invalidation.
9. marks_audit_log
Immutable audit record capturing all manual marks modifications with user attribution.
10. template_design_audit_log
Tracks layout overrides and visual styling changes from Marksheet Layout Studio.
11. auth_security_audit_log
Security event monitoring logging successful logins, failed attempts, and account lockouts.

5. Biannual Backlog Tracking & Automated Re-appear Merging

January & July Cycle

Examinations operate biannually in January and July. Failed courses automatically queue the student into the forthcoming examination roster. Passed courses are carried forward as exemptions (Ex.) upon re-examination.

6. Longitudinal Sem 1–8 Rollup Engine & Final Delivery

Semester 8 Statement

Upon approval of the 8th Semester examination, the longitudinal engine retrieves historical SGPA and Percentage metrics for Semesters I through VII and automatically constructs the Final Academic Evaluation Statement.

7. Institutional RBAC Access Matrix & Security Hardening

Defense-in-Depth

Access control enforces strict separation of duties. Passwords use bcrypt hashing, sessions use HttpOnly cookies to mitigate XSS risks, and consecutive failed logins trigger automatic temporary lockouts.

Brute-Force & Account Lockout Policy

Triggered on 5 consecutive failed login attempts. The account is set to locked_until = NOW() + INTERVAL '15 minutes' and returns HTTP 423 Locked.

Token Invalidation on Password Change

Updating a user's password automatically increments their token_version, instantly revoking all active JWT sessions across devices.
